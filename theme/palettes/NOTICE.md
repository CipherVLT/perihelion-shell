# Palettes: where they come from

Generated by `theme/import-eh-palettes.py` from Event Horizon's settings app
(`src/ux/settings2/theme_presets.zig`, commit `0e596b02167e1f112884e3b9e9dff796c955b0d4`). Do not edit by hand; re-run the importer.

- **Event Horizon** (GPL-3.0),
  https://github.com/Event-Horizon-desktop-environment/Event-Horizon: the
  palettes as Event Horizon ships them, 16 colour roles each. By Event
  Horizon's own note, 13 are written out by hand and the other 255 are
  derived from a seed colour, so most are Event Horizon's palettes named
  after the schemes, not copies of the schemes' own terminal colours.
  These files are a conversion of Event Horizon's data and are offered under
  the same licence, GPL-3.0 (like the rest of Perihelion Shell, see LICENSE).
  Monokai and the palettes named after it are left out: Monokai publishes no
  open licence.
